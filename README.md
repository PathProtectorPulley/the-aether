## About

The Aether is Minecraft's original sky dimension, and still its best. Build a glowstone portal, fill it with water, and step into floating islands of holystone and aercloud, where the fall is the real threat. Bronze dungeons, valkyries, moas to breed and ride, and a full gear tree from skyroot to gravitite await above the clouds.

---

## Features

- A complete floating-island dimension with its own ores, trees and weather
- Bronze, Silver and Gold dungeons, each guarded by a distinct boss
- Moas — hatchable, rideable mounts with breed-dependent jump counts
- Full tool and armour progression: skyroot, holystone, zanite, gravitite
- Accessories slots for rings, pendants, capes and gloves

## Installation

1. **Download** the JAR matching your Minecraft version from the table above.
2. **Install a mod loader** — Forge, Fabric or NeoForge for your exact game version.
3. **Place the `.jar`** into your `.minecraft/mods/` folder (create it if it is missing).
4. **Launch** Minecraft using the modded profile.
5. Verify **The Aether** appears in the in-game mods list.

> **Tip:** if the game crashes on startup, you almost certainly mixed a Legacy JAR with a Modern
> Minecraft version, or are missing a required dependency. Check `latest.log` first.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I make the portal?**
A: Build a nether-portal frame from glowstone and fill it with a water bucket.

**Q: How do I get Moa eggs?**
A: They drop from Gold Dungeon loot and from Moa nests on the islands — hatch them in an incubator.

**Q: What happens if I fall?**
A: You return to the Overworld, usually from a great height. Bring water or a way to break the fall.

**Q: Is it safe to add to an existing world?**
A: Yes in most cases — back the save up first, as you should with any mod addition.

**Q: Can I use it in a public modpack?**
A: Yes. Both builds are modpack-ready and require no additional setup.
